Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

"But How Do It Know?" is an educational video channel. We run a small, private publishing tool ("Cyrex Publisher") that uploads our own videos to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and reads the performance statistics of those videos. This policy explains what that tool accesses.

Who can use the tool

Only the channel's own team. It is not offered to the public, and no outside user can sign in to it.

What we access

  • Basic profile information of our own accounts (account ID, username), used to confirm we are posting to the right account.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to our own accounts.
  • Statistics about our own videos (views, watch time, audience retention), used to improve future episodes.

What we do not collect

We do not collect, store or process personal data about viewers or any other users. We do not sell or share data, run ads through the tool, or use tracking cookies on this website.

How data is stored

Access tokens and downloaded statistics are stored only on the team's own computer, with access restricted to the account owner. Tokens can be revoked at any time from each platform's account settings, which immediately ends the tool's access.

Data deletion

Because the tool stores only data about our own accounts, there is no third-party user data to delete. If you believe we hold any data about you, email buthowdoitknow@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and we will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The tool is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
